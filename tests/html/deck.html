Q3 highlights

  • Revenue reached $18.4M, up 12% on last year and 3% ahead of plan
  • Online orders grew faster than stores for the third quarter running, and now make up 41% of sales
    • Mobile checkout drove most of the gain
    • Returns held steady at 6.2%
  • Gross margin improved to 46.5% as discounting eased
3

What went well, and what to fix

Went well

The autumn range sold through early. Waterproof jackets and trail shoes led the quarter, and the new sizing guide cut exchanges by a fifth.

  • Two new stores opened on time
  • Delivery promise met on 97% of orders

To fix

Stock ran short in the north-west region for six weeks. Customers waited longer for replacements, and support queues doubled at the peak.

  • Rebalance stock weekly, not monthly
  • Hire four seasonal support staff
