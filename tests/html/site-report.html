Renovation site report, week 38

Prepared by the site office, 21 September 2026

Summary

Work on the east wing stayed on schedule this week. The roof membrane is complete, the new windows are in on the ground floor, and the electrical first fix passed inspection on Thursday.

Roof and windows

The roofing team finished the membrane on Tuesday and the flashing on Wednesday. Rain on Thursday gave a useful test: no leaks were found in the plant room or the corridor below.

Window frames for the ground floor arrived complete and were fitted in two days. The first floor frames are due next Monday.

Scaffolding on the north face comes down once the gutters are fitted, expected by the end of next week.
