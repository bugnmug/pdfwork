SERVICES AGREEMENT

Effective 1 October 2026

This Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made between Harbour Analytics Ltd, a company registered in England (the “Supplier”), and Meridian Retail plc (the “Customer”), each a “Party”.

1. Definitions

1.1“Services” means the data analysis and reporting services described in Schedule 1, as changed from time to time under clause 4.
1.2“Deliverables” means all reports, dashboards and other materials the Supplier provides to the Customer in performing the Services.
1.3“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in England.

2. Term

2.1This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues for twelve months, unless ended earlier under clause 6.
2.2The Parties may extend the term by written agreement at least thirty Business Days before it ends.

3. Fees and payment

3.1The Customer shall pay the fees set out in Schedule 2. The Supplier shall invoice monthly in arrears.
3.2Invoices are payable within thirty days of receipt. If the Customer disputes an invoice in good faith, it shall:
(a)tell the Supplier in writing within ten Business Days, giving reasons;
(b)pay the part of the invoice that is not disputed; and
(c)work with the Supplier in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.
3.3Late payments carry interest at four per cent a year above the Bank of England base rate, from the due date until payment.

4. Changes

4.1Either Party may propose a change to the Services. No change takes effect until both Parties sign a written change note that sets out the effect on the fees and timetable.

5. Confidentiality

5.1Each Party shall keep the other’s confidential information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement. This clause survives for three years after the Agreement ends.

6. Termination

6.1Either Party may end this Agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within twenty Business Days of notice of the breach.
6.2On termination the Customer shall pay for all Services performed up to the date of termination.
