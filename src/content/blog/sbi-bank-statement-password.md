**The monthly account statement SBI emails you opens with the last five digits of your registered mobile number followed by your date of birth as DDMMYY.** With mobile 98765 43210 and a birth date of 5 March 1990, the password is `43210050390` ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/sbi-statement-password)).

SBI sends statements through several channels, and they don't all use the same password. Find yours below.

## SBI statement passwords by type

| Statement | Password | Example | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly e-statement by email | Last 5 digits of registered mobile + date of birth as DDMMYY | `43210050390` | [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/sbi-statement-password) |
| Statement requested by SMS | The 4-digit code you chose in the SMS | `2468` | [SBI](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/information-services/kyc-guidelines/sbi-quick-missed-call-banking) |
| Statement from the YONO app | Date of birth as DDMM + `@` + last 4 digits of mobile | `0503@3210` | [TaxHeal](https://www.taxheal.com/?p=123700) |
| SBI Card (credit card) | Date of birth as DDMMYYYY + last 4 digits of the card | `050319904321` | [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/credit-card/sbi-credit-card-statement.html) |

Only the SMS row comes from SBI itself. The others are widely reported by guides, and some sources disagree, especially for SBI Card statements. The email that carried your statement is the final word.

## Getting the email e-statement right

1. Take your mobile number as registered with SBI: 98765 **43210**. Use the last five digits: `43210`.
2. Write your date of birth as day, month and two-digit year: 5 March 1990 becomes `050390`.
3. Join them with nothing in between: `43210050390`.

Common mistakes: using a mobile number that isn't the one registered with SBI, writing the year in four digits (`05031990`), or putting the month first.
