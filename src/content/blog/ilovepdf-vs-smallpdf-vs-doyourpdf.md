**Use DoYourPDF when the document is private or you've hit a free limit: all 75 tools are free, with no limits, and your file never leaves your device. Use iLovePDF or Smallpdf when you need what they have and DoYourPDF doesn't: phone and desktop apps, signature requests to other people, or PDF translation.**

## When DoYourPDF is the better choice

- **The document is private.** Aadhaar, PAN, bank statements, salary slips and contracts never leave your device, so there is nothing to delete later. [Why that matters](/blog/is-it-safe-to-upload-pdfs-online).
- **You've hit a free limit.** There are no task, file or page limits, and nothing is stamped on your files.
- **Your paperwork is Indian.** GST invoices, POS receipts and GST filing summaries, redaction that finds Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN and UPI IDs, and guides to the passwords Indian banks and departments use.
- **Your connection is slow.** Nothing has to travel to a server and back, so a file opens the moment you choose it.

## When iLovePDF or Smallpdf is the better choice

- **You want an app.** Both have phone and desktop apps; DoYourPDF runs in the browser only.
- **Other people need to sign.** Both can send a document for signature and track it. DoYourPDF lets you sign yourself, but doesn't send requests.
- **You need translation.** Both translate PDFs; DoYourPDF doesn't yet.
- **The file is huge and your phone is small.** Server-based tools don't depend on your device's memory, although their free plans cap file sizes.

## Check the claims yourself

You don't have to take any site's word for where your file goes. Open a DoYourPDF tool, switch off your internet, and run it: it still works, because nothing needed to be uploaded. Try the same with an upload-based site and it can't. The meter in DoYourPDF's top bar also counts every byte of your files sent anywhere, and it stays at 0 B.
