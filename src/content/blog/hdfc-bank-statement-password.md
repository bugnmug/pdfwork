**An HDFC Bank account statement opens with your customer ID. An HDFC credit card statement opens with the first four letters of your name in capitals followed by the last four digits of your card.** RAHUL KUMAR, customer ID 12345678, card ending 4321, types `12345678` for the account and `RAHU4321` for the card.

## Account statements

| What you need | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Your customer ID | `12345678` |

Your customer ID is the number you use to log in to HDFC Bank NetBanking. This format is reported by [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/hdfc-bank-statement-password); the email that carried your statement confirms it.

## Credit card statements

| What you need | Example |
| --- | --- |
| First 4 letters of your name, in capitals, as printed on the statement | `RAHU` |
| Then the last 4 digits of your card | `4321` |
| Password | `RAHU4321` |

In a reply to a customer, HDFC Bank said the name to use is the one on the statement, not the one embossed on the card. For some recently reissued cards it gave the date of birth as DDMM instead of the card digits: `RAHU0503` for someone born on 5 March ([HDFC Bank's reply](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/hdfc-bank-unable-to-open-the-credit-card-attachment-sent-to-my-mail-id-c1628833)).

## If it still won't open

- **Capitals.** `rahu4321` fails; type `RAHU4321`.
- **Short first names.** A first name of three letters runs into the surname: ANU THOMAS gives `ANUT`.
- **The wrong statement type.** Account and card statements use different formats. Check which one you have.
- **Read the email.** HDFC Bank's statement emails say which format applies.
- **Download it again** from NetBanking or the HDFC Bank app if the email is lost.

## Make an unlocked copy

Loan apps and tax filing often need the statement without a password. Once you know it:

1. Open [Remove Password](/remove-password) and choose the statement.
2. Type the password once.
3. Download the unlocked copy. The statement is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

To get the transactions into a spreadsheet, run the unlocked copy through [PDF to Excel](/pdf-to-excel). Other banks are covered in [bank statement PDF passwords](/blog/bank-statement-pdf-password).
